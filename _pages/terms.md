## About this site

This personal academic website shares research information and NMR tutorials. Software instructions describe the examples shown; check the documentation for the version you use. Linked papers and software retain their respective licenses and terms of use.

## Privacy and external services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads some resources from external services, including mathematical typesetting and an existing Google Analytics script. Loading these resources sends requests to those providers. External links take you to sites with their own privacy practices.

For questions about this website, contact **henrik[at]usp.br**.

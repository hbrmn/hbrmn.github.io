Lithium-disilicate glass can reorganize before clear changes appear in its silicate network. Lithium NMR and molecular dynamics reveal changes in lithium environments and spatial distributions during sub-transition-temperature relaxation. Complementary NMR measurements then detect early crystallization, providing a way to distinguish structural relaxation from crystal formation.

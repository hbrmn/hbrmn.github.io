A combination of NMR experiments maps the local environments and atomic proximities in sodium aluminofluorophosphate glasses. The results distinguish phosphorus-bound and aluminum-bound fluorine and show how these species interact with sodium. Together, the measurements provide a structural model that goes beyond the information available from individual spectra.

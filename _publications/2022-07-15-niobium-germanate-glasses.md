Niobium-93 NMR distinguishes local environments in europium-doped niobium germanate glasses. Increasing niobium content favors more niobium–oxygen–niobium connections, while heat treatment changes the structure and europium luminescence. The study links these atomic environments to optical behavior and demonstrates laser-written waveguides in the glasses.

Mixing germanate and tellurite networks affects lithium and sodium conductivity differently. NMR, Raman spectroscopy, and conductivity measurements reveal a small enhancement for lithium, while sodium shows little departure from the endmember trend. The comparison tests how modifier identity influences ion transport in mixed-network glasses.

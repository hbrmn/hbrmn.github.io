This study examines niobium incorporation into rare-earth-doped aluminophosphate glasses using optical measurements, Raman spectroscopy, and multinuclear solid-state NMR. The results link increasing niobium content to changes in phosphorus and aluminum environments and in the optical properties of the glasses.

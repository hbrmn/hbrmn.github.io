Multinuclear NMR disentangles the effects of bismuth and fluorine in fluoride–phosphate glasses for optical applications. Fluoride can terminate phosphate chains or remain associated with metal ions, while increasing bismuth fluoride content changes sodium’s local surroundings. These distinctions clarify how the additives reorganize the glass network.

How can REDOR measure atomic proximity when the recoupled nucleus is quadrupolar? Simulations and model compounds establish suitable conditions for lithium-6 experiments. Applied to borophosphate glasses, the method reveals stronger lithium interactions with phosphate than with borate groups, illustrating why formal charge alone does not predict modifier distributions.

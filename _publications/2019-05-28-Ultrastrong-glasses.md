Why does magnesium improve crack resistance in aluminoborosilicate glasses? Multinuclear NMR links this behavior to changes in boron coordination and cation preferences for charge compensation. The findings explain the mechanical response through redistribution of existing structural units, rather than the formation of entirely new network motifs.

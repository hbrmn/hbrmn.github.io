Neutron and X-ray diffraction are combined with multinuclear NMR to resolve the structure of glasses in the sodium titanium phosphosilicate NASICON system. The results show a fully connected silica component, preferential association of sodium with phosphate groups, and titanium environments that differ from ideal octahedral coordination.
